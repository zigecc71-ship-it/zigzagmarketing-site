WHY GHOSTWRITING

You have things to say. You just don't have time to write.

People buy from people they trust. Regular, thoughtful posts build that trust, but writing is the first thing to fall off the to-do list.

The problem: a blank page and a full calendar

You know what you want to say, but a good post takes an hour or two. So you post once a month, or not at all.

The result? Your expertise stays invisible, while the people who post consistently get seen.

Our approach: you talk, we write

In a short conversation we draw out your stories, opinions and experience. We turn that into writing in your tone and style.

You just read it, tweak it if you want, and approve.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Will it really sound like me?

That's the whole point of the service. Before we write anything, we study how you speak and write and capture your voice in a profile. We refine the first drafts together until you're happy.

Is the writing done by AI?

AI helps us with research and first drafts so we can work faster. The substance, ideas and stories are yours, and a person edits and reviews every piece before it comes to you for approval.

How much of my time do you need?

About an hour at the start for an intro conversation. After that, a short chat every few weeks and a few minutes to approve drafts.

Will anyone know I'm not writing it myself?

Not from us. The work is fully confidential, and we're happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement (NDA).

Which languages do you write in?

Slovenian and English.
