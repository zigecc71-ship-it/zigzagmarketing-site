WHY ADS

Your ads aren't bad. They're just too similar.

Meta no longer targets by interests. It targets by what's in your ads. Most D2C brands give it too little variety to work with, so growth stalls.

The problem: 30 ads that Meta sees as 1

Meta's Andromeda algorithm groups similar ads (same product photo, same template, different headline) into one cluster. One cluster gets a single ticket to the auction.

The result? 30× the work for 1× the opportunity. Your one winning ad burns out, ROAS drops and you start over.

Our approach: more genuinely different concepts

Every week we put new angles into testing: social proof, comparisons, before/after, objections, offers and founder stories.

Done by hand, that's slow and expensive. With our AI system, we produce in a few days what traditional production would take a month to deliver.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Will the ads look like "AI ads"?

No. We use your brand identity, your real product photos and the tone your customers know. A person reviews every ad before it goes live.

Why static ads and not video?

Video has its place, but it's slower and more expensive to produce. Static ads are the fastest way to give the algorithm lots of different concepts and find winning messages quickly. You can later carry those into video too.

Do you also run the ad account, or only create the ads?

Either. We can create ads for your in-house team or agency, or take over testing and scaling your campaigns as well.

What budget do I need?

We work both with brands just starting to advertise and with brands already spending several thousand euros a month on Meta. With smaller budgets, we make sure every ad you test teaches you something new instead of competing with copies of itself.

How fast can we start?

After a short call and an account review, we usually have the first batch of ads ready for testing within a few days.
