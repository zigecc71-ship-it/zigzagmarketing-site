WHY A NEW SITE

A good-looking site isn't the same as a site that sells.

Most websites are built for the owner, not the customer. Visitors can't tell within a few seconds what you offer and why they should choose you, so they leave.

The problem: visitors arrive and leave

You pay for an ad, someone clicks, and they land on a slow page with an unclear message and too many options.

The result? Expensive clicks that never turn into enquiries or sales.

Our approach: one page, one goal

We start with the message: what your customer worries about, what they want and why they should trust you. Only then do we design.

Every part of the page leads to one step: an enquiry, a purchase or a booking.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take?

It depends on the scope and how quickly the content is ready. A landing page usually takes one to two weeks, a larger website a few weeks. You'll get an exact timeline in the proposal.

How much does a new site cost?

The price depends on the number of pages, features and languages. After a short call you get a fixed quote, with no surprises.

Can I edit the site myself later?

Yes. If you want to edit content yourself, we build the site on a system that allows it and show you how to use it.

Where will the site be hosted?

With the provider you already use, or we help you set up hosting, a domain and business email.

Do you redesign existing sites too?

Yes. We first review what's working today and what isn't, then suggest a redesign or targeted improvements.
